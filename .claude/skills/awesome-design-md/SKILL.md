# Awesome DESIGN.md

Source: https://github.com/VoltAgent/awesome-design-md (MIT, see LICENSE).

Each folder in `design-md/<brand>/` holds a `DESIGN.md`: palette, typography, spacing,
components and layout rules taken from that brand's website.

## How to use

1. List what is available: `ls design-md/`.
2. Pick one or two references that fit the client's trade and audience (an auto service can
   borrow structure from `bmw`, `bmw-m`, `ferrari` or `dji`; a calm local business from `airbnb`
   or `apple`). Read their `DESIGN.md` fully.
3. Take the system (type scale, spacing, how sections and buttons are built, how photos are
   used), not the brand itself: never copy another company's logo, name, colours as a whole
   identity, or wording. The client's own colours and photos win.
4. Write down in the plan which reference was used and what was taken from it.
