# Rapid mod — design spec for walls, drills, mech units

This is the source of truth for the content added on top of the turrets. Numbers and visual
design here are final; the generator code (`tools/`) implements them.

Everything stays **pure hjson + PNG** (no Java, no JS) so the mod keeps working on iOS.
Everything under `content/`, `sprites/`, `bundles/` is generated by `python3 tools/gen.py`.

## 0. Format facts (checked against the v146 game classes — do not guess beyond these)

- Mod name is `rapid-turret`; content name = `rapid-turret-<file name>`; sprite region name =
  `rapid-turret-<png name>`. References inside hjson may use the short name (`pellet`, `rapid-wall-1`).
- hjson: one value per line. A quoteless string runs to the end of the line, so arrays of
  `item/amount` MUST be one entry per line. Colours are written quoted: `"ffbe50"`.
- `research: <parent>` or `research: { parent: <name>, requirements: [ ... ] }` puts content in the tech tree.
- Block classes used: `Wall`, `Drill`, `UnitFactory`. Unit `type: mech`.
- `Wall` fields: `lightningChance, lightningDamage, lightningLength, lightningColor, chanceDeflect, flashHit`
  (+ `Block`: `health, size, armor, absorbLasers, insulated, requirements, category`).
- `Drill` fields: `tier, drillTime, liquidBoostIntensity, rotateSpeed, drawMineItem, drawRim, drawSpinSprite,
  hardnessDrillMultiplier, warmupSpeed`. Regions: `<name>` (base), `<name>-rotator`, `<name>-top`, `<name>-item`
  (drawn last, tinted with the ore colour).
- `consumes: { power: <float>, liquid: { liquid: water, amount: <float>, optional: true, booster: true } }`.
- `UnitFactory`: `plans: [ { unit: <name>, time: <ticks>, requirements: [ ... ] } ]`.
  Regions: `<name>`, `<name>-out` (drawn rotated, opening on the RIGHT side at rotation 0), `<name>-top`
  (drawn over the unit under construction), `<name>-in`. Ship all four.
- Mech unit regions: `<name>` (body), `<name>-cell` (team colour, drawn tinted), `<name>-base` (hips),
  `<name>-leg` (ONE leg, same canvas as the body, placed on the right half; the game mirrors it).
- Weapon: `name: <x>` → sprite `<x>.png` (the parser prefixes the mod name). Weapon sprite is centred on
  its mount point and points UP. Weapon fields used: `name, x, y, top, mirror, alternate, reload, recoil,
  shootY, inaccuracy, ejectEffect, bullet`.
- The game generates the dark outline for **turrets, units and weapons** itself. Those sprites are drawn
  WITHOUT an outline and keep a transparent margin (5px turrets, 4px units/weapons).
  Walls, drills and the factory are full-tile blocks: no outline is generated, they fill the whole canvas.
- Sprites: 32px per tile, 4px = 1 world unit. Units and guns face UP.
- Effects known to exist: `casing1 casing2 casing3 shootSmall shootBig shootSmallSmoke shootBigSmoke
  hitBulletSmall hitBulletBig`.

## 1. Shared visual language

Pixel art, hard edges, no anti-aliasing, left/right mirror symmetry, light from the top
(top edge of every plate 1px lighter, bottom edge 2–3px darker) — exactly what `Art.plate` in
`tools/gen.py` does. Reuse that helper.

Tier colours (same as the turrets):

| Tier | accent | metal (light plate) | steel (dark plate) |
|------|--------|---------------------|--------------------|
| 1 | (255,190,80) orange | (152,154,164) | (110,112,128) |
| 2 | (90,215,255) cyan   | (146,158,176) | (98,108,132)  |
| 3 | (120,255,150) green | (150,164,156) | (96,114,110)  |
| 4 | (255,110,215) pink  | (160,152,172) | (104,94,124)  |
| 5 | (255,84,60) red     | (168,150,148) | (92,76,80)    |

**Emblem** — the mod's mark, used on walls, drill frames and the forge: two stacked chevrons
pointing up (`^` over `^`), drawn in the accent colour, 1px stroke on 32px art, 2px on 64px+.
Each chevron is `w` wide and `w/2` tall; the second sits `w/2 + 1` below the first.

## 2. Walls — 10 blocks

`rapid-wall-N` (1x1) and `rapid-wall-N-large` (2x2), N = 1..5. `type: Wall`, `category: defense`.
Large = 4x health and 4x cost of the small one.

| N | health/tile | armor | cost per tile | extra |
|---|-------------|-------|---------------|-------|
| 1 | 420  | 0 | copper 6, graphite 2 | — |
| 2 | 640  | 0 | titanium 6, silicon 3 | — |
| 3 | 920  | 2 | thorium 6, titanium 4 | — |
| 4 | 1250 | 4 | plastanium 5, thorium 5 | `absorbLasers: true`, `insulated: true` |
| 5 | 1700 | 6 | surge-alloy 6, phase-fabric 3 | `lightningChance: 0.05`, `lightningDamage: 25`, `lightningLength: 17`, `lightningColor: accent`, `chanceDeflect: 10`, `flashHit: true` |

Research: `rapid-wall-1` ← `copper-wall`; `rapid-wall-N` ← `rapid-wall-(N-1)`;
`rapid-wall-N-large` ← `rapid-wall-N`.

**Sprite (32px small / 64px large), fills the whole canvas:**
1. Whole tile = steel, darkened 0.7 on the outermost 1px ring (2px on large) so neighbouring walls read as separate plates.
2. Inset plate (inset 2px small / 4px large) in metal with the standard bevel; corners chamfered by 2px / 4px.
3. Inner recessed panel (inset 8px small / 16px large) in steel, bevel inverted (dark top edge, light bottom edge) so it reads as sunk in.
4. Emblem centred in the panel: `w` = 8px small, 16px large.
5. Rivets: 1px (2px large) dots in metal×0.62 at the four corners of the metal plate, 4px (8px) in from the edge.
6. Tier 4 adds a 1px (2px) accent line along the top and bottom inner edge of the panel (energy shielding look).
   Tier 5 adds that line on all four sides of the panel.
7. Large walls only: 1px dark seam cross through the metal plate (not through the panel), so it looks like four armour tiles.

`rapid-drill-N`. `type: Drill`, `category: production`.

| 3 | 3 | 4 | 230 | 1.5 | 0.09 | 4.5 | copper 60, graphite 50, silicon 45, titanium 35 |

**Sprites (S = size × 32), four layers on the same canvas:**

- **Base `rapid-drill-N.png`** (opaque, full tile): steel housing with a 1px (2px for S ≥ 128) darker outer ring;
  chamfered metal frame plate inset 2px; a round **well** in the centre, radius 0.40·S, filled with
  steel×0.45, with a 2px steel×0.62 lip around it; four corner blocks keep the metal frame. On each corner block:
  one rivet and a short accent tick (L-shape for N ≥ 3). Emblem (`w` = S/6) on the bottom-centre frame edge only if
  the frame there is at least `w/2 + 6` px tall — otherwise skip it.
- **Rotator `-rotator.png`** (transparent background): rotor centred in the canvas, radius 0.36·S.
  N blades by tier: 1→3, 2→4, 3→4, 4→6, 5→6. Each blade is a tapered wedge (wide at the hub, about 0.10·S at the tip
  ... i.e. narrow at the tip, 0.16·S wide at the hub) in metal with a lighter leading edge and a darker trailing edge,
  plus a small accent tip (last 2–4px). Must look the same after rotating by 360°/blades.
- **Top `-top.png`** (transparent background): octagonal hub, radius 0.15·S, metal with bevel, a dark round
  socket in its centre (radius 0.07·S, steel×0.45). For N ≥ 3 add a thin steel ring (2px) at radius 0.20·S around the hub.
- **Item `-item.png`** (transparent background): pure white diamond (rotated square), half-diagonal 0.06·S,
  centred — it sits inside the socket and the game tints it with the ore colour.

T3: `canDrown: false`.

Weapon mount (world units; `x` sideways, `y` forward) = half the body width + 1, i.e. `x`: 4.75, 5.75, 7.5, 12, 15.5;
`y`: 0.5, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2; `shootY` = half the gun sprite height / 4 − 1.

# Part 2 — conveyors and air units

Same rules as above (pure hjson + PNG, generated by `python3 tools/gen.py`, same visual language and tier colours).

## 6. Format facts for this part (checked against the v146 sources)

- `Conveyor` fields: `speed`, `displayedSpeed` (+ `health`, `requirements`, `category: distribution`).
  Regions: `<name>-<v>-<f>` for shape `v` = 0..4 and animation frame `f` = 0..3 — **20 PNGs of 32×32 per conveyor**.
  The game rotates/flips them itself; all are drawn for a belt whose OUTPUT is on the RIGHT edge:
  - `0` straight: input from the left.
  - `1` curve: input from the TOP edge, output right.
  - `2` input from the left AND the BOTTOM edge.
  - `3` input from the left, top AND bottom.
  - `4` input from the top AND bottom edges, nothing from the left.
  Frames advance in the direction of travel; the block icon is `<name>-0-0`.
- Flying unit: `type: flying` plus `flying: true` (the flag is not implied by the type). Regions: `<name>` (body) and
  `<name>-cell` only — no base, no legs. Engine flames are drawn by the game at `engineOffset` world units behind the
  centre with radius `engineSize`. Other fields used: `speed, accel, drag, rotateSpeed, health, armor, hitSize,
  lowAltitude, circleTarget, engineOffset, engineSize, weapons`.
- A weapon whose sprite does not exist is simply not drawn (the gun is then painted into the body sprite).
  A weapon with a sprite may set `rotate: true` and `rotateSpeed` to become a turning turret; it is drawn on top of the body.

## 7. Conveyors — 5 blocks

`rapid-conveyor-N`, `type: Conveyor`, `category: distribution`.

| N | speed | displayedSpeed | health | cost per tile |
|---|-------|----------------|--------|---------------|
| 1 | 0.05  | 7  | 50  | copper 1, graphite 1 |
| 2 | 0.085 | 12 | 70  | copper 1, lead 1, titanium 1, silicon 1 |
| 3 | 0.115 | 16 | 100 | titanium 1, silicon 1, thorium 1 |
| 4 | 0.15  | 21 | 140 | plastanium 1, thorium 1, silicon 1 |
| 5 | 0.19  | 26 | 190 | plastanium 1, surge-alloy 1, phase-fabric 1 |

Research: `rapid-conveyor-1` ← `conveyor`, then each from the previous tier.

**Sprites (32×32, coordinates x right, y down, origin top-left):**

Parts:
- **Belt**: steel×0.5, fills everything that is not rail, nub or cut corner.
- **Rail**: 4px thick strip of metal; its outermost row/column is metal×0.7, the row/column next to the belt is metal×1.2.
  Every rail carries static accent segments: 6px long, 2px thick (the two middle rows of the rail), one per 16px,
  starting 5px from the rail's start. Rails never animate.
- **Nub**: a 4×4 metal square in a corner (outer row and column metal×0.7).
- **Arrow**: a chevron 5px long and 9px across, 2px stroke, colour = steel×0.9 mixed toward the accent by
  0.25 / 0.35 / 0.5 / 0.65 / 0.8 for tiers 1..5. Arrows are the only thing that animates: each frame moves every arrow
  4px further along its lane; arrows repeat every 16px along a lane, so frame 4 would equal frame 0.
  An arrow is clipped to the belt (never drawn over rails or nubs).

Shapes:
- **0 straight**: rails along the whole top (y 0–3) and bottom (y 28–31). One lane left→right along y = 16; arrows point right,
  arrow tips at x = 6 + 4f and x = 22 + 4f (wrap around at 32 so the pattern is seamless between tiles).
- **1 curve**: rail along the whole LEFT edge (x 0–3) and the whole BOTTOM edge (y 28–31); the bottom-left corner is cut:
  the triangle below the line from (0,22) to (9,31) is fully transparent and the rail follows that diagonal (4px thick).
  Nub in the top-right corner. Lane: down the column x = 16 from the top edge to y = 16, then right along y = 16 to the right
  edge (total 32px, two arrows 16px apart, offset 4f). Arrows on the vertical leg point down, on the horizontal leg point right.
- **2**: rail along the whole top only. Nubs in the bottom-left and bottom-right corners. Main lane exactly as in shape 0.
  Side lane: up the column x = 16 from the bottom edge to y = 22; arrows point up; one arrow, tip at y = 30 − 4f
  (hidden once it would pass y = 22).
- **3**: no rails; nubs in all four corners. Main lane as in shape 0, the side lane of shape 2, and its vertical mirror from the top
  (arrow pointing down, tip at y = 1 + 4f, hidden once it would pass y = 9).
- **4**: rail along the whole LEFT edge. Nubs in the top-right and bottom-right corners. Two side lanes as in shape 3 (from top and
  bottom, meeting in the middle), and a right-pointing lane along y = 16 from x = 16 to the right edge (one arrow, tip at
  x = 18 + 4f, wrapping at 32 back to 16).

Tier flavour: tier 4 and 5 rails get a second accent segment per 16px (so the segments read as a dashed light strip).
Tier 5 additionally has a 1px accent line along the belt edge of each rail.

## 10. Format facts for this part (all checked with `javap -p [-c]` on the v146 `server.jar`)

- All classes used are in `mindustry.mod.ClassMap` (so `type:` resolves): `PowerTurret, ItemTurret, ShieldWall, MendProjector,
  LaserBoltBulletType, BasicBulletType, ShootSpread, ShootAlternate, ConsumeCoolant`.
- `PowerTurret` has ONE bullet: `shootType: { type: ..., ... }` (no `ammoTypes`). Power: `consumes: { power: <float per tick> }`
  (shown in game ×60). Its fire rate scales with power satisfaction. Draws exactly like `ItemTurret`.
- Turret regions (`DrawTurret`): `<name>` (gun, outlined by the game), `<name>-heat`, `<name>-base`. Nothing else is shipped.
- A turret calls `shoot()` at most once per tick, so `reload: 1` = 60 volleys/s is the ceiling; more comes only from `shoot.shots`.
- `Turret.shootEffect/smokeEffect` are null by default → the bullet's `shootEffect/smokeEffect` are used, drawn in the bullet's `hitColor`.
  `Turret.consumeAmmoOnce` is true by default: a `ShootSpread` volley of N pellets uses ONE ammo unit.
- `ShootSpread`: `shots`, `spread` = degrees BETWEEN neighbouring pellets (fan = (shots−1)·spread, centred on the aim).
- `LaserBoltBulletType` (extends `BasicBulletType`): `width`, `height`, `backColor`, `frontColor`. Its defaults are green
  (`hitLaser`), so `hitEffect`, `despawnEffect`, `smokeEffect` are overridden below.
- `ShieldWall` (extends `Wall`): `shieldHealth, breakCooldown (ticks), regenSpeed (shield points per tick), glowColor, glowMag, glowScl`.
  Region `<name>-glow` (drawn additively in `glowColor` while the shield is up). Damage hits the shield first; at 0 the shield is off
  for `breakCooldown`. The shield is also off while the block has no power. The shield itself is drawn by the game (team-coloured square).
  `conductivePower: true` (a `Block` field) makes touching power consumers share one power graph.
- `MendProjector` (NOT a `Wall`): `reload, range, healPercent, phaseBoost, phaseRangeBoost, baseColor, phaseColor`. Regions `<name>`,
  `<name>-top` (drawn tinted `baseColor`, pulsing alpha ≤ 0.5). Every `reload` ticks it heals each damaged friendly building whose
  centre is within `range + that building's size·4` world units — itself included — by `healPercent` % of that building's max health.
  **With no consumers its "optional boost" counts as always active**, i.e. `phaseBoost` and `phaseRangeBoost` are always added → both
  MUST be set to 0. `Wall`'s constructor defaults are copied by hand: `group: walls`, `priority: -2` (a float in this build),
  `buildCostMultiplier: 6`, `crushDamageMultiplier: 5`, `canOverdrive: false`.
- A regenerating wall as a true `Wall` subclass does not exist in v146 (no heal field on `Wall`/`Block`); `RegenProjector` needs a
  drawer and is Erekir-only, so `MendProjector` with a 2-unit range is the pure-hjson way.
- Names verified to exist — `Fx`: `none, hitLaserColor, shootSmallColor, shootBigColor, shootSmall, shootBig, shootSmallSmoke,
  shootBigSmoke, hitBulletSmall, hitBulletBig, casing2, casing3`. `Sounds`: `lasershoot, shotgun`. Items: `scrap, lead, graphite, thorium …`.
- Colours with alpha are 8 hex digits, quoted: `"ffbe5080"`.

## 19. Format facts for this part (checked with `javap -p -c -constants` on the v146 `server.jar` and the v146 sources)

- Unit type string is **`type: naval`**. `ContentParser` maps it to `UnitWaterMove::create` (bootstrap method #11 of the
  `flying/mech/legs/naval/…` switch; any other string throws "Invalid unit type").
- **Do NOT write `naval: true`, `canDrown` or `omniMovement`.** `UnitType.init()` sets them itself when the constructed unit is a
  `WaterMovec`: `naval = true`, `canDrown = false`, `omniMovement = false`, adds the `wet` immunity and the naval path cost.
  (This is the opposite of the air units, where `flying: true` had to be written.) The source comment on `naval` says "do NOT assign this manually".
- Regions loaded and drawn for a naval unit: `<name>` (body) and `<name>-cell` only. `-base`/`-leg` are drawn only for `Mechc`. No engine flames.
- Wave trail (drawn by `UnitWaterMove` under the unit, coloured from the water floor, only while on a liquid floor): two trails that start
  at `(±waveTrailX, waveTrailY)` world units from the centre (`waveTrailY` negative = behind), `trailLength` points long (0 = none, the
  default), width `trailScl`. All four are `UnitType` fields.
- `faceTarget: false` (a `UnitType` field, default true) — every vanilla ship sets it; the hull keeps its heading and only the turrets turn.
  Therefore **every naval weapon here has a sprite and `rotate: true`**.
- Speed on water: a `UnitWaterMove` moves at `speed × 1.3` on deep water and `speed × 1` on shallow water. It cannot leave liquid floors.
- `Weapon.mirror` defaults to **true** → centreline turrets (`x: 0`) MUST write `mirror: false`. A non-mirrored weapon fires once per `reload`.
- Multi-barrel centreline turret: `shoot: { type: ShootAlternate, barrels: <n>, spread: <world units between barrel centres> }`
  (`ShootAlternate.barrels`, `.spread`; same class the Pulse turrets use). Shot `i` leaves at `(i mod n − (n−1)/2) · spread` sideways.
- Independent anti-air mount: `controllable: false` + `autoTarget: true` (`Weapon` fields; `autoTarget` only works when `controllable` is false).
  Such a weapon picks its own target with `findTarget(…, bullet.collidesAir, bullet.collidesGround)`, so it follows the bullet's flags.
- `FlakBulletType` (in `ClassMap`, extends `BasicBulletType`): its constructor sets `collidesGround = false` → flak mounts target and hit
  **air only**. Fields used: `damage, speed, lifetime, splashDamage, splashDamageRadius, width, height, frontColor, backColor, hitEffect`
  (+ its own `explodeRange`, default 30, left alone).
- Vanilla `naval-factory` is a plain `UnitFactory` with exactly one extra field: **`floating: true`** (`Block.floating`). `Build.validPlace`:
  a tile on deep water is refused unless `floating` (or `requiresWater`/`placeableLiquid`) is set, and the block must touch at least one
  non-deep tile (inside it or on its edge ring) — so "on shallow water or on deep water next to the shore". `UnitFactory` itself has no water logic.
  Vanilla does NOT forbid building it on land. `requiresWater: true` exists (`Block.requiresWater`: every covered tile must have
  `liquidDrop == water`) but is a pump rule, is not what vanilla uses, and is **not used** here.
- A finished ship leaves the factory only if the tile one world unit in front of the exit is a liquid floor and not solid
  (`UnitPayload.dump` → `EntityCollisions.waterSolid`). The exit side must face water; otherwise the unit stays inside (vanilla behaviour).
- Research parent block name is `naval-factory` (`Blocks.navalFactory = new UnitFactory("naval-factory")`).
- Names verified to exist — `Fx`: `flakExplosion, flakExplosionBig, hitBulletSmall, hitBulletBig, casing1, casing2, casing3`.
  `Sounds`: `shootSnap`. `UnitType`: `faceTarget, trailLength, waveTrailX, waveTrailY, trailScl, accel, drag, rotateSpeed, hitSize, armor`.
  `Weapon`: `name, x, y, top, mirror, alternate, rotate, rotateSpeed, controllable, autoTarget, reload, recoil, shootY, inaccuracy,
  ejectEffect, shootSound, shoot, bullet`.
- Not possible in pure hjson and therefore not used: abilities with constructor arguments (`ShieldRegenFieldAbility` has no no-arg
  constructor), forbidding land placement of the factory without `requiresWater`.
- Vanilla ship sprites are not square (risso 70×78 … omura 264×351) — non-square unit canvases are fine (`Art(W, H)`).

## 24. Implementation notes

1. Unit hjson starts with `type: naval` and `faceTarget: false`; it must NOT contain `naval`, `flying`, `canDrown`, `engineOffset`, `engineSize`.
2. Every centreline weapon writes `x: 0` and `mirror: false` (the default is true and would double the turret). `alternate` is written only on mirrored flak.
3. `shoot` is an object, one key per line: `type: ShootAlternate`, `barrels`, `spread`. The skiff has no `shoot` block.
4. Flak entries: `controllable: false`, `autoTarget: true`, bullet `type: FlakBulletType` with NO `collidesGround` key (the class default false is the point).
5. Weapon order in the file = draw order: main turrets bow → stern, then flak. On the dreadnought the second turret (`y: 11`) is drawn over the
   rear of the first — intended (superfiring).
6. Sprite files — per unit `sprites/units/<name>.png`, `<name>-cell.png` (W×H), `<name>-turret.png`, and for T2–T5 `<name>-flak.png`;
   factory `sprites/blocks/rapid-shipyard.png`, `-top.png`, `-out.png`, `-in.png` (160×160). No `-base`, `-leg`, `-gun` for ships.
7. `forge.hjson()` needs two new optional arguments: `health` (1800 here) and `extra` lines (`floating: true`), written after `size`.
8. `tools/validate.py` additions:
   - `CLASSES["naval"] = "mindustry.type.UnitType"`; weapon keys against `mindustry.type.Weapon`; bullet keys against the class named by the
     bullet's own `type` (`FlakBulletType`); `shoot` keys against `mindustry.entities.pattern.ShootAlternate`; effect and sound names against
     `Fx` / `Sounds` (already implemented for Part 3 — extend it to unit weapons).
   - every naval file: has `faceTarget: false`, has none of the keys of note 1, every weapon has `rotate: true`, every weapon with `x == 0`
     has `mirror: false`, every `FlakBulletType` weapon has `controllable: false` and `autoTarget: true`.
   - `rapid-shipyard.hjson`: `floating` is true, `requiresWater` absent, research is `naval-factory`, 5 plans whose units are the five ships.
   - sprites: body and cell are W×H of section 20, turrets/flak the canvases of 22.4/22.5, all with an empty 4 px border and mirror symmetric;
     body row 4 has 2..4 opaque pixels (bow tip), row `yb` has `2·sw − 2 .. 2·sw` (flat stern) and row `ys` has `2·hw`; the body pixel at every weapon mount
     `(cx ± 4x, cy − 4y)` is opaque; every cell pixel lies on a `dark` body pixel's position; `shootY` = (sprite height/2 − 4)/4;
     `spread × 4` = the distance between neighbouring barrel centres of 22.4.
   - bullet `speed × lifetime` equals the range column ±1; bundles have `.name` and `.description` for the 5 units and the shipyard in EN and RU.
9. Add the ships (body + cell + turrets and flak composited at their mounts, `outlined()`) and the shipyard to `tools/preview.py`;
   add `metaglass: 2` to `VALUE` if `--balance` should print the table of section 20.

## 25. Performance pass, level 3 (gameplay changes, visuals kept)

Chosen by the player from the Android performance analysis. Bullet trails, smoke and casings are kept on purpose.
- **Regen walls removed from the game** (sections 14, 15.4 are obsolete): every one was a `MendProjector` that updates and draws each
  frame; hundreds of them dropped a host to 8 FPS. Saves that contain them lose those blocks on load.
- Rapid VI: `shots: 1` instead of 2 at `reload: 1`, all ammo damage ×2 (60 bullets/s instead of 120, same DPS);
  surge ammo `lightning: 1` with `lightningDamage = 2·damage/3` (same lightning damage per hit).
- Silicon ammo (Rapid I–VI): no homing (`homingPower`/`homingRange` searched for a target every tick per bullet); damage +10 %.
- Buckshot IV: 5 pellets (spread 9°, same 36° fan) instead of 7, pellet damage ×7/5; the gun sprite has 5 bores.
- T4–T5 units (barrage, storm, tempest, hurricane, frigate, dreadnought): main guns `reload` at least 7, damage scaled to keep DPS.
