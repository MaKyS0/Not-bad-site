# Рубеж — sound credits

Every recorded sound in the game is embedded (base64 MP3, mono) in `../sounds.js`. Each clip was cut,
peak-normalised to −1 dBFS, high-passed at 30 Hz, faded out and re-encoded from the source listed below.
All sources are **CC0 (public domain dedication)** as stated on their OpenGameArt pages (checked
2026-11-14). Credit is not required by CC0 but is given here with thanks. No audio from any commercial game is used.
Sounds without a suitable CC0 recording (shell casings, UI, stingers, distant echo tails) are synthesised in `audio.js`.
